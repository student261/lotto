🏪 Store Worker Quick Guide — 5 Simple Steps
📦 1. When Lottery Delivery Comes to Store
Click the blue "Stock Intake / Update Inventory" button on the right side.
Scan the barcode on the back of the lottery pack. The system automatically reads the game title, ticket price, and adds it to your inventory!
🎟️ 2. Putting Tickets into a Box (e.g. Box 62 or 63)
Click on any empty dispenser box (or scan the box barcode).
Select the pack from inventory, enter the starting ticket number, and click Activate. The system will announce: "Box ready to sell"!
🛒 3. Selling Tickets to Customers
When a customer buys tickets, simply point your scanner at the ticket barcode, or click the +1 button on that box.
The system advances the ticket count and calculates sales revenue automatically.
💵 4. Ending Shift & Balancing Cash Drawer
Click "End Shift" or "Settlement" at the top.
Type any Winning Payouts (Cashes) paid to customers, enter your drawer starting cash float, and count your physical cash.
The system verifies if your drawer is Balanced, Over, or Short. Then click "Start New Shift" for the next worker!
📄 5. Official Georgia Lottery Day Report
Click "Day Report" in the top menu to view and print the official 2-page Georgia Lottery retail report matching the lottery terminal layout.
